Plain steps first
Health is shaped by the conditions around a person, not only by personal choices.

MindBridge turned the dense paragraph into a lesson you can finish: one big idea, clear steps, an everyday story, and a quick check.

Plain steps

  1. Start with the big idea: life conditions shape health.
  2. Name the conditions: money, school, housing, food, rides, safety.
  3. Connect the path: conditions change stress, risk, and access to care.
  4. End with policy: rules decide who gets help and how hard help is to reach.

Story version

Sam has asthma. His apartment has mold, the bus is unreliable, and the clinic is across town. His illness is medical, but the reasons it keeps getting worse are built into his daily conditions.
